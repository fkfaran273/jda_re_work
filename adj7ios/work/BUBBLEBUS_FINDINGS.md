## Incidental finding: Android session identity

Our first Android batch returned ONE adid (`f81a2cff…`) for three different
GAIDs with `400 request check interval failed`. Re-tested in isolation with
32-hex GAIDs: **3 distinct adids, all 200**. So sessions are device-unique;
the 400s were the ~20-min per-adid session throttle, not an identity bug. A
real SDK's `primary_dedupe_token` is not required for uniqueness.

## Remaining hypotheses (none proven)

1. **Server-side click acceptance.** AGENTS already records this wall: a
   click that will book echoes `click_id=` in the 302; ours never does. That
   needs a mobile-carrier IP shared by click+session, or a real device click.
   Our egress is a datacenter IP — though berain accepted the same IP, so
   this is not a blanket block.
2. **Campaign-side config.** Paused/capped, or an os/device filter that
   excludes our traffic. We cannot see campaign settings.
3. **Unknown publisher requirement.** The real flow may pass something the
   tracker needs (a whitelisted sub-param) that we are not sending.
