## The law (short form)

```
header   = MINSTD(second): x=(sec mod 2^31-1, 0->1); 8 draws 16807*x; w_i=d2i^d2i+1; BE x4
record   = 01 00 08 | d0head(16, phase) | S(BE32) | 03 | D9(20 zero B) | 04 | BLOCK
           | 05 | sha256("") | 06 | 02 | 07 | device_name(16) | 0e*14
BLOCK    = sha256_iv2(D9 || 00 08 || head16=record[3:19] || LE32(S) || sha256("")
                    || TAIL(17B: 02||device_name||00*6) || claims)
claims   = adj_verification || pool<session_count || "1500000" || pool>=session_count
           || activity_kind, client_sdk, headers_id, native_version
whitening= 14-round AES-derived SPN (inverse ShiftRows, custom KB), CBC from header
digest   = sha256((K^opad)||opad||sha256((K^ipad)||ipad||body))
```

Notes:
- `head` (the d0 selector phase) is device-state and NOT server-verified — any
  phase is accepted live (proven with (0x40,) vs the capture's (0x09,)).
- host follows ATT: att_status=3 → consent.adjust.com; 0 → analytics.adjust.com.
- `adj_verification` = BASE64(cycle19("Quoh8uafohgh7CahM2h") XOR {"os_name":...});
  real device = `KlcAG2cbAAsKSl1KXiwSSjA=`, this service = `…RT5bBSQZDhxXB0Mb`.
- Open items: click-engagement binding via pure HTTP (see adj7 findings —
  fraud-suite IP or AdServices token); rec3 background-push claims exception.
